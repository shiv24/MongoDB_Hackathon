1 Request

Talk to the PromptForge agent. It pulls your project's context from Atlas and writes a cited brief.

    2 Brief

    Brief exported. It was downloaded as promptforge-brief.md and copied to your clipboard. In your project folder, run: 
    claude "Implement the brief in promptforge-brief.md. Follow every requirement and decision, and check each acceptance check."

    3 Result

    After your coding tool builds it, record which acceptance checks the result actually meets. Checks you don't mark stay "not run".

      4 Feedback

      Tell the agent what was wrong. It suggests a lesson and asks who it applies to. Or write it here. Nothing is remembered until it's approved.
